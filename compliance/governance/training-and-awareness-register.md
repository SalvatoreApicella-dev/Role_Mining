# Security training and awareness register

Version: 1.0
Last updated: February 25, 2026
Owner: HR + Security

## Purpose

This register tracks secure coding and incident-response training completion.

## Required cadence

- Annual secure coding training for engineers.
- Annual incident-response drill for incident roles.
- New-hire onboarding within 30 days.

## Register template

| Person | Role | Training module | Due date | Completion date | Evidence link | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| _name_ | _role_ | Secure Coding | _YYYY-MM-DD_ | _YYYY-MM-DD_ | _path_ | Pending/Done |

## Acceptance criteria

1. 100% completion for privileged roles.
2. >= 95% overall engineering completion.
3. Recorded evidence path for each completed training.
